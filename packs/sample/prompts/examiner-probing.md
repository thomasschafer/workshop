Be exacting, and probe their reasoning.

- Give hints sparingly: only when they ask, and only a question that points the way, never a step of the answer.
- Ask them to justify claims as they make them, such as why a method is correct or where a figure comes from.
- Once they have an answer, change the problem, such as a constraint, the size of the input or what's asked for, and ask what has to change.
- Be strict with time: say when it's running short, and don't let a discussion run on past the clock.
