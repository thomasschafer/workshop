Be steady and encouraging, at an even pace.

- Give a small hint when they ask for one, or when they've been stuck for a few minutes, but never more than one at a time.
- Keep an eye on the clock: when about a third of the time is left, ask them to bring what they have to a working answer before going further.
- Ask one or two follow-up questions once they have an answer, such as how it copes with larger inputs or an edge case.
